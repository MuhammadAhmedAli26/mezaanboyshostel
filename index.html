Peace & Discipline

Hostel Rules & Resident Code of Conduct

To ensure an orderly, secure, and peaceful atmosphere for every student and professional, all residents agree to observe the following community standards.

01

Night Gate Timings

For your personal safety and overall hostel security, the main gate strictly closes at 10:30 PM. Residents working late shifts or with university evening classes must obtain a written late-pass authorization from the warden.

02

Visitor & Guest Policy

Guests and day visitors are welcomed solely in the designated ground-floor reception and guest lounge until 8:00 PM. Unauthorized overnight stays, outside persons in bedroom corridors, and female visitors are strictly prohibited.

03

Zero Tolerance for Substance Abuse

Mezaan Boys Hostel is a completely smoke-free, tobacco-free, and drug-free facility. Smoking cigarettes, vaping, narcotics, alcoholic drinks, or possession of hazardous materials triggers immediate, non-negotiable expulsion without fee refund.

04

Quiet Study Hours

Silent study hours are enforced from 11:00 PM to 7:00 AM. Loud speakers, shouting in corridors, noisy gaming, or any disruptive behavior that disturbs roommates and fellow residents is not allowed.

05

Cleanliness & Room Care

Keep your personal beds, wardrobes, and study desks organized. Avoid littering in washrooms or hallways. Tampering with electrical wiring, electrical heaters, or heavy unauthorized appliances is strictly forbidden for fire safety.

06

Dues & Advance Notice

Monthly hostel dues must be cleared within the first 5 days of each calendar month. A minimum 15 to 30 days prior notice is required before vacating your room to claim the security deposit.
